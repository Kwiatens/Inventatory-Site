## Locate a part

Open **2 Racks**, select a rack, then choose a slot in its **5 × 5 grid**. Use the slot details to inspect its component. Stock also shows the selected component's rack location.

## Place and move components

Use **Actions** for rack creation, assignment, automatic assignment, unassignment, printing, filtering, and other rack administration.

For an occupied slot, **Place/Move** starts a move. Select the destination and complete the move there. Check the resulting location before leaving the rack.

An empty rack shows **Delete rack**, also available with `x`. Confirm with `Enter` to remove it. The deletion is recorded in History.

## Pick parts for a board

Open a project and choose **Find in racks** with `f`. Inventatory visits one rack at a time, highlights the needed slots, and lists the current picks. `Enter` advances; `Backspace` returns to the previous step.

Parts outside racks appear last, grouped by location. At the end, confirm whether to subtract the picked quantities from stock. See [Projects and BOMs](../projects/) for availability and shortage handling.

## Physical rack resources

These instructions cover rack locations inside the software. A public fabrication or assembly package was not verified for this website; no printable hardware files are offered here.
